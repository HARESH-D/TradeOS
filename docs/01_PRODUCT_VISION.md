# Product Vision

Mission: Help Indian swing traders become more disciplined and profitable through broker data, analytics, risk awareness, journaling, and AI-assisted review.

Target users: Indian retail swing traders.

Primary broker: Angel One SmartAPI.

TradeOS is not a broker, a price prediction app, or a trading bot. It is an intelligent trading workspace that answers practical questions:
- How much capital is available?
- How much risk is already taken?
- Was the trading plan followed?
- What are the real profits after brokerage, STT, GST, exchange charges, SEBI charges, and stamp duty?
- Which setups, sectors, symbols, and behaviors are actually profitable?
- Which mistakes repeat over time?

## Product Pillars

- Broker integration through adapters
- Portfolio and charges engine
- Risk and rule engine
- Dashboard and analytics workspace
- Trading journal
- AI coach over the user's own trading history
- Future order automation with strong guardrails

## Current Build Priority

The first usable product slice is:
- Trading dashboard
- Manual broker sync
- Analysis grid with spreadsheet-style column filters

Journal, AI coach, strategy validation, replay mode, multi-broker expansion, and order automation are future milestones.
